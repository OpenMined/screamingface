# H. Brendan McMahan
**Role:** Senior Staff Research Scientist, Google Research
**Category:** ABC Citations — P13

## Who They Are
H. Brendan McMahan co-invented federated learning — the framework that enables ML models to be trained across decentralized devices without raw data ever leaving the client. His 2017 paper "Communication-Efficient Learning of Deep Networks from Decentralized Data" is one of the most cited papers in the ML privacy literature and is the direct technical antecedent to the federated infrastructure the ABC thesis argues should underpin public AI. He works at Google Research and is primarily a technical researcher with limited public-facing engagement — no books for general audiences, no regular media presence. His value in this context is not advocacy but technical sign-off: if the privacy and attribution claims in screamingface's architecture hold up to his scrutiny, that carries weight with exactly the researchers who read the footnotes.

## Relationship to AI Tools
A builder of infrastructure rather than a user of applications. McMahan's work happens at the framework level — he builds the systems that make privacy-preserving computation tractable, rather than building on top of them. His engagement with screamingface, if it happened, would be a technical peer review rather than a product endorsement.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
McMahan would engage narrowly and technically. The federated learning angle — distributing AI compute and data across participants rather than centralizing it — is directly relevant to the infrastructure layer the ABC thesis proposes. He'd be interested in whether the ensemble routing architecture is compatible with proper federated training at scale, and whether the privacy guarantees match what federated learning can formally provide. He wouldn't blog about it or write an op-ed, but a citation from his lab or a collaborative technical paper would signal to the federated learning research community that the ABC framework is worth taking seriously.

## Key Tension
The ABC thesis cites McMahan's foundational work as evidence that decentralized AI infrastructure is technically feasible — but there is a significant gap between federated learning as a training framework and screamingface as a routing layer, and bridging that gap requires the public investment and institutional coordination the thesis calls for but doesn't yet demonstrate.
