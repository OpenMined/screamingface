# Yoshua Bengio
**Role:** Professor, Université de Montréal; Founder and Scientific Advisor, Mila (Quebec AI Institute); Co-President and Scientific Director, LawZero
**Category:** ABC Citations — P10

## Who They Are
Yoshua Bengio is a Turing Award–winning deep learning pioneer who has, since roughly 2023, become the most prominent establishment AI researcher to publicly pivot toward AI safety and governance advocacy. As founder of Mila — one of the world's leading AI research institutes — and now through LawZero, a nonprofit focused on safe-by-design AI, he occupies a rare position: technical credibility at the frontier combined with a genuine, widely-publicized concern about AI power concentration and existential risk. His public statements have grown increasingly pointed about the danger of AI capability concentrating in two or three countries or companies, framing it as a structural civilizational risk.

## Relationship to AI Tools
A builder and researcher first — Bengio has spent his career advancing the very capabilities he now advocates caution about. He doesn't reject AI tools; he uses them and funds their development. His current lens is safety-by-design: what architectural and governance choices made during development determine whether systems are safe and accountable, rather than patched after the fact.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
Bengio would be philosophically aligned and practically cautious. The decentralization argument — that open ensemble infrastructure creates an alternative to single-company AI dominance — maps directly onto his public concerns about power concentration. His LawZero mission of "safe by design" finds a technical analog in the attribution-based control framework the tool is built around. He'd likely be supportive in principle, but his concern would be whether routing through frontier corporate APIs constitutes real structural decentralization or just a convenient UI layer on top of the same underlying power structure. He might engage through a blog post, a co-signed statement, or a citation in a policy submission rather than as a direct user.

## Key Tension
If the underlying models are developed and controlled by a small number of corporations, does an open routing layer meaningfully advance the "advanced AI as a global public good" goal Bengio now champions — or does it require the deeper infrastructure investment the ABC thesis argues for?
