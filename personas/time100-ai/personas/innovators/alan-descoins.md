# Alan Descoins
**Role:** CEO, Tryolabs
**Category:** Innovator

## Who They Are
Alan Descoins is a self-taught programmer from Uruguay who has spent 15 years building Tryolabs from an early employee into its CEO — leading an AI consultancy that works with Nvidia, Hyundai, and Ulta Beauty while also building mission-driven AI for UNICEF (heatwave indicators), solar power forecasting, and satellite-based rural school identification. He has deep engineering roots and a practitioner's perspective on what AI tools actually deliver in production versus what they promise in demos.

## Relationship to AI Tools
Practitioner-engineer who evaluates and deploys AI tools across dozens of real client projects; he has a 15-year calibrated view of which AI claims hold up in production.
