# Alex Blania
**Role:** Co-founder and CEO, Tools for Humanity
**Category:** Innovator

## Who They Are
Alex Blania co-founded Worldcoin (now World) with Sam Altman to solve what he sees as the defining problem of the AI era: how do you prove you're a human in a world of highly capable AI agents? His company deploys iris-scanning Orbs to create verified human identity credentials (World ID), with 33 million users by September 2025. He believes that within years, thousands of humans will fall "deeply in love" with AI, and sees provable human identity as critical infrastructure for the AI economy.

## Relationship to AI Tools
Technical founder deeply engaged with the intersection of AI capabilities and human identity verification; he thinks about AI from a societal infrastructure perspective.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
Blania would find the credit-sharing mechanism interesting from an identity and attribution perspective — who gets credit for what AI output is exactly the kind of problem his work touches. He'd ask pointed questions about how screamingface verifies contributor identities in the credit-sharing network.

## Key Tension
His current focus is identity verification infrastructure at planetary scale; screamingface's credit-sharing model would interest him as a use case for World ID but doesn't yet integrate with his stack.
