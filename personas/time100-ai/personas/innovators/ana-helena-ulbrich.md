# Ana Helena Ulbrich
**Role:** Co-founder and Director, NoHarm
**Category:** Innovator

## Who They Are
Ana Helena Ulbrich is a Brazilian pharmacist who co-founded NoHarm.ai with her brother Henrique in 2019 out of frustration at reviewing 200 prescriptions per day as a hospital pharmacist. NoHarm is a nonprofit AI system that reviews clinical prescriptions for dangerous drug interactions — processing 5 million prescriptions per month across 200+ hospitals in Brazil and reaching 2.5 million patients, entirely free to the Brazilian public health system (SUS). NoHarm has doubled its reach every year since 2019.

## Relationship to AI Tools
Domain-specific AI deployer in a life-safety context; her AI work is applied and mission-driven, and she thinks about AI accuracy and reliability in terms of patient outcomes.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
Ulbrich would be warmly appreciative of the open-source and democratization ethos — NoHarm's free-to-public-health-system model shares the same spirit. But her AI context is clinical and life-safety-critical; she'd be cautious about the non-deterministic nature of ensemble model routing in any safety-sensitive context. For general-purpose developer productivity she'd be supportive.

## Key Tension
Her hardest-won lesson is that AI in high-stakes domains requires rigorous validation against real outcomes — she'd want to see whether screamingface's benchmark claims translate to real developer productivity before endorsing it.
