# Andy Parsons
**Role:** Senior Director of Content Authenticity, Adobe
**Category:** Innovator

## Who They Are
Andy Parsons leads Adobe's Content Authenticity Initiative (CAI) — a project that has grown to 5,000+ member organizations and developed Content Credentials, a digital signature standard that embeds provenance metadata (creator identity, edit history, AI usage disclosure) permanently into media files. Content Credentials have been adopted by Google, OpenAI, TikTok, LinkedIn, Amazon, Samsung, and all five major camera manufacturers.

## Relationship to AI Tools
Standards builder and open-source advocate — the CAI's tools are open-source, and Parsons has built a genuinely cross-industry coalition around technical standards for AI content attribution.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
Parsons would find the credit-sharing mechanic in screamingface directly relevant — tracking which AI model generated which output, attributing contributions across an ensemble, and making that provenance visible are exactly the problems CAI is solving for media. He'd likely be enthusiastic and might even see integration opportunities between Content Credentials and screamingface's output tracking.

## Key Tension
His standards work is focused on content provenance for media files; adapting it to code generation and CLI output is a novel but tractable extension of the same problem.
