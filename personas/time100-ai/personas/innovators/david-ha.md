# David Ha
**Role:** Co-founder and CEO, Sakana AI
**Category:** Innovator

## Who They Are
David Ha led Google Brain Japan before co-founding Sakana AI in Tokyo in 2023 with Llion Jones (a Transformer co-author). Backed by Nvidia, NEC, and Fujitsu, Sakana's research philosophy is inspired by natural systems — emergent intelligence from small, collaborative agents rather than monolithic large models. In March 2025, Sakana's AI Scientist became the first AI system to have a paper accepted by peer reviewers at a major ML conference.

## Relationship to AI Tools
Research scientist and technical founder who builds novel AI architectures and training paradigms; a genuine AI researcher who happens to be a CEO.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
Ha would find the ensemble routing concept directly aligned with Sakana's research philosophy — the idea that combining smaller, specialized models outperforms any single large model is basically Sakana's founding thesis. He'd be enthusiastic and intellectually engaged, might want to contribute research on optimal ensemble composition, and would see Sakana's models as natural candidates for inclusion in the routing layer.
