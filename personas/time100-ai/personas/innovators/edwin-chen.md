# Edwin Chen
**Role:** Founder and CEO, Surge AI
**Category:** Innovator

## Who They Are
Edwin Chen is the former Facebook/Google/Twitter ML engineer who bootstrapped Surge AI in 2020 to $1 billion+ in revenue with under 100 employees — one of the fastest companies in history to reach that milestone without external funding. Surge works with 1 million+ contractors to create high-quality training datasets for Google, Anthropic, OpenAI, and others, differentiating on complex agentic behavior training data rather than simple labeling.

## Relationship to AI Tools
Deep technical ML practitioner and builder who has worked at the data layer that makes frontier models possible; he understands model training, evaluation, and capability benchmarks from first principles.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
Chen would be immediately engaged and probably the most technically credible evaluator of screamingface's benchmark claims on this list. His background in RLHF and model evaluation gives him the tools to assess whether the routing logic actually works as claimed. He'd try it, probe the methodology, and if convinced, might become an advocate — the open-source ethos aligns with his bootstrapped independence.

## Key Tension
His data labeling business partly depends on AI models having distinct capability profiles — if all models converge to ensemble performance, his competitive positioning of "complex behavioral training" becomes harder to market.
