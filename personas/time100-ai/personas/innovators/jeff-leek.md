# Jeff Leek
**Role:** Vice President and Chief Data Officer, Fred Hutchinson Cancer Center
**Category:** Innovator

## Who They Are
Jeff Leek is a biostatistician and VP/CDO at Fred Hutchinson Cancer Center who is leading the Cancer AI Alliance (CAIA) — a coalition of cancer research centers and tech companies using federated learning to share AI insights across institutions without sharing patient data. He is also co-founder and co-CEO of Synthesize Bio and holds the J. Orin Edson Foundation Chair of Biostatistics. He is known in the data science community for his public communication about statistics and advocacy for rigorous, reproducible data science.

## Relationship to AI Tools
Practitioner-researcher who builds and deploys AI systems in biomedical research contexts; he uses AI tools in data science workflows and has strong opinions about methodological rigor and reproducibility.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
Leek would be a thoughtful and potentially enthusiastic evaluator — his data science background makes him appreciate benchmark-driven tool selection, and his open-source orientation aligns with screamingface's ethos. His research teams write code, and an ensemble coding assistant that provably improves output quality would be directly useful.

## Key Tension
His strongest pushback would be methodological: how were the SOTA benchmark claims validated, what's the confidence interval, and has the evaluation been peer reviewed? He'd want the science behind the routing claims to meet the same rigor he applies to his cancer AI research.
