# Kakul Srivastava
**Role:** CEO, Splice
**Category:** Innovator

## Who They Are
Kakul Srivastava led Adobe's $9B+ Creative Cloud business and served as GitHub's VP of Product before becoming CEO of Splice — the sample library and music creation platform with millions of musicians. She has integrated AI tools into Splice's creation workflow while building a framework that explicitly compensates and credits the creators whose work trains the models. She has called push-button AI creation "insulting to musicians."

## Relationship to AI Tools
Creative product executive who deploys AI tools in creative workflows at platform scale; she thinks about AI from a creator rights, user experience, and platform business model perspective.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
Srivastava would find the credit-sharing mechanic compelling — it's analogous to Splice's model of compensating sample creators when their work is used. The open-source ethos and ensemble approach mirror her thinking about AI tools as augmentation rather than replacement.

## Key Tension
Her most pressing question would be about the compensation model: who in the credit-sharing network gets attributed when ensemble routing produces a good output, and how is that attribution tracked?
