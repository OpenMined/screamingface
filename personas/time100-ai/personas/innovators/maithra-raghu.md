# Maithra Raghu
**Role:** Co-founder and CEO, Samaya AI
**Category:** Innovator

## Who They Are
Maithra Raghu is a Cambridge mathematics graduate and Cornell CS PhD who spent seven years as a Google Brain research scientist studying how deep neural networks represent and learn knowledge. She co-founded Samaya AI in 2022 to build AI-powered knowledge discovery for financial services, raising $43.5 million with backers including Eric Schmidt, Yann LeCun, and Marty Chavez.

## Relationship to AI Tools
Research scientist turned founder who thinks at the level of how models learn and represent knowledge; she builds bespoke AI systems for high-stakes institutional use cases rather than using general-purpose tools.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
Raghu would be intellectually engaged with the ensemble routing methodology — her research background makes her want to understand the benchmark evaluation rigor, model selection criteria, and whether routing decisions are theoretically grounded or empirically driven. She'd likely identify gaps in the evaluation framework that could be strengthened.

## Key Tension
Her world is specialized institutional AI (finance); screamingface's general-purpose coding CLI would be a curiosity, not a use case.
