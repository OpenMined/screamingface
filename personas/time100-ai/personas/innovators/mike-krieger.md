# Mike Krieger
**Role:** Chief Product Officer, Anthropic
**Category:** Innovator

## Who They Are
Mike Krieger co-founded Instagram with Kevin Systrom, scaled it to a billion users as CTO, and departed Facebook/Meta in 2018 before co-creating the AI-powered news app Artifact. In May 2024 he joined Anthropic as CPO, responsible for Claude's product surface including enterprise applications, Claude.ai, and the API developer experience. He brings a rare combination of consumer-scale product intuition (Instagram), AI application building (Artifact), and deep alignment with Anthropic's safety mission.

## Relationship to AI Tools
Builder and practitioner — he has shipped software at Instagram scale, built AI products as a founder, and now owns the product roadmap for one of the most-used AI models in the world. He thinks in products, not infrastructure.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
Krieger would be directly and personally interested — screamingface routes through Claude as one of its primary models, and its developer community overlaps heavily with Anthropic's API customer base. He'd want to understand the routing logic, benchmark methodology, and developer experience with a product-manager's analytical eye. He might even try it.

## Key Tension
As Anthropic's CPO, he's accountable for Claude being used well and safely — a tool that routes Claude prompts based on benchmark performance without knowledge of Anthropic's intended use policies would make him want to engage with the OpenMined team directly.
