# Mitesh Khapra
**Role:** Associate Professor, IIT Madras
**Category:** Innovator

## Who They Are
Mitesh Khapra is an IIT Madras professor who co-leads AI4Bharat — a research lab that has taken researchers to nearly 500 of India's 700 districts to record thousands of hours of voices across all 22 official Indian languages, building the open-source datasets, models, and tools that power India's national Bhashini AI translation initiative. AI4Bharat's models are deployed in the Indian Supreme Court for document translation and in voice bots for rural farmers.

## Relationship to AI Tools
Academic ML researcher and open-source practitioner who builds production-quality AI infrastructure for underrepresented languages; his work is deeply practical, mission-driven, and open-source by design.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
Khapra would be genuinely aligned with screamingface's open-source ethos — AI4Bharat is built on the same principle that AI capability should be democratized and not locked behind commercial APIs. He'd likely be interested in whether screamingface's ensemble routing could eventually include multilingual coding models or handle non-English code comments and documentation well.

## Key Tension
His immediate concern would be India-relevance: does screamingface's benchmark methodology and routing logic perform equitably for developers coding in Hindi, Tamil, or Telugu contexts, or is it optimized for English-language coding workflows?
