# Natasha Lyonne
**Role:** Co-founder, Asteria Film Co.
**Category:** Innovator

## Who They Are
Natasha Lyonne is the Emmy-nominated actress and creator of Russian Doll who co-founded Asteria Film Co. — a production company powered by Moonvalley, an AI video model trained exclusively on licensed content. She is directing her debut feature "Uncanny Valley" using a combination of AI and traditional filmmaking techniques, and has become one of Hollywood's most vocal advocates for ethical AI in creative production. She is sharply critical of training data that's "super kosher copacetic to rob freely under the auspices of acceleration."

## Relationship to AI Tools
Creative practitioner who uses AI directly in production workflows; her relationship to technology is aesthetic and ethical, not technical — she cares about how AI changes the creative process and whether it respects creators' rights.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
Lyonne would have genuine curiosity about an open-source AI tool built with an ethos of transparency and credit sharing — the "share AI credits with friends" mechanic resonates with her interest in equitable AI ecosystems. But screamingface's developer focus would make it feel like a different world from her creative filmmaking context.

## Key Tension
She's focused on AI ethics in creative industries; screamingface's coding CLI world is adjacent to her concerns about AI training data provenance but doesn't directly address the creator compensation issues she's fighting for.
