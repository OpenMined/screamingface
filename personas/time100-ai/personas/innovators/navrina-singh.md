# Navrina Singh
**Role:** Founder and CEO, Credo AI
**Category:** Innovator

## Who They Are
Navrina Singh founded Credo AI in 2020 to build the enterprise AI governance category — providing platforms that help organizations identify risks (security, compliance, bias) in their AI systems as they scale generative and agentic AI. She advises the U.S. Department of Commerce's National AI Advisory Committee, the UN, the OECD, and the World Economic Forum. Credo AI quadrupled revenue and doubled its customer base (Mastercard, McKinsey, Northrop Grumman) in 2025.

## Relationship to AI Tools
Governance and risk practitioner who has built technical AI evaluation tools and sits at the intersection of policy and engineering; she understands AI systems deeply through an accountability and risk lens.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
Singh would be immediately interested in the governance and accountability questions screamingface raises: how is the routing decision made, who is responsible when an ensemble produces a harmful output, and how do enterprise organizations audit multi-model systems? She'd see screamingface as a test case for the governance frameworks Credo AI builds.

## Key Tension
The lack of out-of-the-box governance, audit logging, and compliance controls in an open-source CLI tool would be a gap she'd push hard on before recommending it to Credo AI's enterprise clients.
