# Priya Donti
**Role:** Assistant Professor, MIT
**Category:** Innovator

## Who They Are
Priya Donti holds the Silverman Family Career Development Professorship at MIT, where her research group develops machine learning algorithms to optimize high-renewables power grids — including a demand forecasting model in use by the UK's national grid. She co-founded Climate Change AI (CCAI), a nonprofit with 16,000 registered summer school participants, to connect the global AI community to climate action.

## Relationship to AI Tools
Academic ML researcher who builds bespoke optimization algorithms for power systems; technically deep in ML but operates at the research-methods level, not general-purpose developer tooling.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
Donti would be intellectually curious about ensemble methods and routing optimization — these are adjacent to her research interests in constrained optimization and multi-objective ML. She'd be supportive of the open-source and democratization ethos. Her research group writes code and screamingface could improve their workflow.

## Key Tension
