# Refik Anadol
**Role:** Artist
**Category:** Innovator

## Who They Are
Refik Anadol is a Turkish-American media artist and UCLA professor who has been "painting with data" since a 2016 Google Artist Residency sparked his vision of machine learning as an artistic collaborator. He describes data as "a pigment — a brush that can think" and creates monumental AI-generated installations for public spaces worldwide. He is building DATALAND — the world's first Museum of AI Arts — opening in Los Angeles in Spring 2026, and his Large Nature Model is an open-source generative AI model trained on ethically sourced natural imagery.

## Relationship to AI Tools
Practitioner who uses custom ML models as a core artistic medium; he collaborates directly with technical teams to build bespoke AI systems, and his open-source LNM reflects genuine commitment to accessible AI tools for artists.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
Anadol would appreciate the open-source ethos and the democratization angle — his view that artists are limited by access to compute, not ideas, aligns with screamingface's framing of distributing AI capability broadly. But his world is creative computation, not coding CLIs; he'd be a curious outside observer rather than a user.

## Key Tension
The DATALAND mission (broadening access to AI arts computing) shares philosophical DNA with screamingface, but their communities don't overlap.
