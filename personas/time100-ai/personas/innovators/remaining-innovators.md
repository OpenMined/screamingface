# Remaining Innovators — Brief Notes

The following innovators were researched but operate in domains (robotics, autonomous vehicles, sovereign AI infrastructure, mineral exploration, wildlife biology, defense) with minimal practical overlap with screamingface's developer coding tool value proposition. Brief notes below.

---

## Peggy Johnson — CEO, Agility Robotics
Deploys humanoid robots (Digit) in Amazon warehouses. Her engineering teams write code and might benefit from screamingface, but her AI focus is on safety-critical physical systems. Would be cautious about non-deterministic model routing in production engineering workflows.

## James Peng — Founder and CEO, Pony.ai
Safety-first autonomous vehicle engineer executing "mass production year" for robotaxis. Thoughtful outside observer; his engineering teams might use screamingface but his strategic focus is entirely on AV deployment scale.

## Tareq Amin — CEO, Humain
Building Saudi Arabia's full-stack sovereign AI company. Signed $23B in deals with Nvidia, AMD, AWS, Qualcomm within months of launch. Screamingface routes through US-centric commercial models, cutting against Humain's sovereignty thesis.

## Mfikeyi Makayi — CEO, KoBold Metals Africa
Leads KoBold's African operations using ML to predict mineral deposits (copper, lithium, cobalt). Highly specialized AI stack for geoscience; respectfully curious about screamingface but not a user.

## Brandon Tseng — Co-founder and President, Shield AI
Former Navy SEAL building AI pilots for military aircraft (Hivemind). His engineering teams building autonomous flight systems have strict security requirements that prevent routing proprietary code through commercial cloud APIs.

## Denise Herzing — Founder and Research Director, Wild Dolphin Project
Has studied the same pod of Atlantic spotted dolphins since 1985; her dataset became training data for Google DeepMind's DolphinGemma. Warm, appreciative observer of screamingface's democratization ethos but not a developer.

## Mati Staniszewski — Co-founder and CEO, ElevenLabs
Built ElevenLabs to $330M ARR and $11B valuation. Voice AI is adjacent but distinct from code generation; would find the ensemble concept interesting as a product design pattern but operates in a different domain.
