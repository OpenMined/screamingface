# Sam Rodriques
**Role:** Co-founder and CEO, FutureHouse
**Category:** Innovator

## Who They Are
Sam Rodriques is a theoretical physicist turned AI researcher who co-founded FutureHouse in 2023 as a nonprofit with the explicit goal of building AI scientists — systems that can formulate hypotheses, design experiments, and analyze results autonomously. In 2025, FutureHouse released a platform of four specialized scientific AI agents, made a first AI-driven discovery (a promising AMD treatment), and released ether0 (an open-weights reasoning model for chemistry).

## Relationship to AI Tools
Deep technical practitioner and architect of AI research systems; he thinks about AI agency, reasoning chains, and scientific validity at the research level.
