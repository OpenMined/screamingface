# Alexandr Wang and Nat Friedman
**Role:** Superintelligence Lab Co-leads, Meta
**Category:** Leader

## Who They Are
Alexandr Wang founded Scale AI at 19 — building the data labeling infrastructure that trains most frontier models — and grew it to a $29 billion valuation before joining Meta as its first Chief AI Officer in June 2025. Nat Friedman is the former GitHub CEO who brought developer-centric product instincts and a history of open-source platform building to Meta's superintelligence effort. Together they lead Meta Superintelligence Labs.

## Relationship to AI Tools
Wang is a technical infrastructure and data layer expert; Friedman is a developer-tools product veteran who shipped GitHub Copilot. Both think about AI from a systems scale perspective.
