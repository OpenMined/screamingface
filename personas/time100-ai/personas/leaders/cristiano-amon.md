# Cristiano Amon
**Role:** President and CEO, Qualcomm
**Category:** Leader

## Who They Are
Cristiano Amon has repositioned Qualcomm from a smartphone chip company into the leading edge AI inference platform across devices, PCs, and IoT. His central thesis — "whoever has presence on the edge is going to win" — frames AI as fundamentally moving from cloud-centric to on-device, with Snapdragon NPUs as the compute layer that makes personal, private, context-aware AI possible.

## Relationship to AI Tools
Deep technical operator who lives at the silicon and systems architecture layer; he cares about how AI models are compiled and run on hardware, not their application-layer ergonomics.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
Amon would find the local model routing concept (Ollama in the ensemble) perfectly aligned with his edge AI thesis. A CLI tool that preferentially routes to on-device Ollama models when available is exactly the developer experience he'd want to see built.

## Key Tension
His commercial interest is in Snapdragon silicon running models locally — the cloud-heavy portions of the ensemble are less interesting to him.
