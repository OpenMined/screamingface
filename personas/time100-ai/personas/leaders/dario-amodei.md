# Dario Amodei
**Role:** CEO, Anthropic
**Category:** Leader

## Who They Are
Dario Amodei left OpenAI's VP of Research role in 2020 to co-found Anthropic with his sister Daniela and a cohort of safety-focused researchers. A PhD physicist by training, he has built Anthropic around the paradox of racing to build the most capable AI while treating safety research as existential. By early 2026, he was warning that AI will disrupt 50% of entry-level white-collar jobs within 5 years, and published essays framing AI safety as humanity's defining challenge.

## Relationship to AI Tools
Deeply technical researcher-CEO who understands model architecture and safety alignment at the research level; he uses Claude himself extensively and cares about how models are deployed.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
Amodei would be cautiously interested — Claude is a first-class model in the ensemble, and screamingface's routing approach aligns with Anthropic's positioning that Claude should be deployed where it's safest and most capable. He'd scrutinize the safety implications of arbitrary model routing and want to understand how Claude's Constitutional AI constraints are preserved in the ensemble context.

## Key Tension
He's expressed discomfort with a few companies controlling AI decisions — screamingface distributes that control further, which he'd find philosophically correct but potentially complicated from a safety accountability perspective.
