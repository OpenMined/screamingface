# David Holz
**Role:** Founder, Midjourney
**Category:** Leader

## Who They Are
David Holz built Midjourney as a bootstrapped, profitable independent research lab with the explicit goal of "expanding the imaginative powers of the human species" — not making art, but expanding imagination. He is deeply philosophical about AI's role as an amplifier of human creativity rather than a replacement for it, and has kept Midjourney intentionally small, community-focused, and independent despite massive commercial pressure.

## Relationship to AI Tools
Creative AI practitioner and researcher who thinks about AI from a human potential and aesthetic lens; he is a maker who uses his own tools.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
Holz would find screamingface interesting in principle — the ensemble concept resonates with his view that no single model is optimal for all tasks, and he values tools that expand what people can do. But Midjourney is image generation; screamingface is coding. The audience overlap is minimal, and he'd likely view it with respectful curiosity rather than practical engagement.

## Key Tension
Holz's creative AI world and screamingface's developer productivity world barely intersect; his indifference would be affable, not hostile.
