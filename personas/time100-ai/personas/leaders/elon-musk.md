# Elon Musk
**Role:** Founder, xAI
**Category:** Leader

## Who They Are
Musk founded xAI in 2023 to build Grok, framing it as a more "truth-seeking" alternative to ChatGPT. He has open-sourced Grok 2.5 and committed to open-sourcing Grok 3, positioning xAI as a transparency-forward player in the US-China AI race. His approach is maximalist: building massive compute clusters (Colossus), forecasting AI will surpass human intelligence, and treating open-source release as both a competitive and ideological move.

## Relationship to AI Tools
Deep technical operator — he engages directly with model architecture decisions, compute scaling, and deployment strategy at xAI while also controlling X as a distribution channel.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
Musk's open-source commitments make him ideologically sympathetic; he's explicitly tried to make Grok available to the developer community. He'd likely be mildly interested in the ensemble routing concept but would prefer xAI/Grok to be a first-class citizen in the routing logic rather than one model among many.

## Key Tension
His competitive instincts mean he'd want Grok to win the routing game outright, not be democratically weighted alongside Claude, Gemini, and Codex.
