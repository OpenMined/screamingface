# Jensen Huang
**Role:** CEO, Nvidia
**Category:** Leader

## Who They Are
Jensen Huang transformed Nvidia from a gaming GPU company into the foundational hardware layer of the AI era, now calling it "the largest infrastructure buildout in human history." He frames AI as infrastructure like electricity — universal, profitable, and in a virtuous cycle where better models drive more investment which improves models further. His focus has shifted toward agentic and physical AI, with Nvidia positioning itself at every layer from training chips to robotics platforms.

## Relationship to AI Tools
Master hardware strategist — he thinks in terms of compute architectures and the software stacks (CUDA, NIM) that lock in developer adoption, not at the CLI or application layer.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
Huang would be broadly supportive of anything that increases AI inference demand at the edge — more models being run means more Nvidia hardware sold. A tool that routes prompts through multiple models is, from his vantage point, simply more compute being consumed.

## Key Tension
He's indifferent to which software layer wins; his interest evaporates unless screamingface drives meaningful GPU demand.
