# Liang Wenfeng
**Role:** CEO, DeepSeek
**Category:** Leader

## Who They Are
Liang Wenfeng is a 40-year-old Chinese quant finance founder who built a $10 billion hedge fund (High-Flyer) on algorithmic trading, then pivoted entirely to AI research in 2023, founding DeepSeek. The January 2025 release of DeepSeek-R1 — a 671B-parameter open-source reasoning model built for $5.6 million on 2,048 Nvidia H800s — stunned the global AI industry. His approach is research-first, efficiency-obsessed, and genuinely open-source.

## Relationship to AI Tools
Research engineer and systems thinker who approaches AI from a quantitative finance rigor perspective — compute efficiency, architectural tradeoffs, and model design with unusual precision for a CEO.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
Liang would be genuinely interested — DeepSeek models are natural candidates for inclusion in the screamingface ensemble given their open weights and benchmark performance. His efficiency-first ethos aligns with the premise that routing to the best model avoids wasteful over-reliance on expensive frontier models.

## Key Tension
DeepSeek's Chinese origin creates geopolitical friction that will affect enterprise adoption of screamingface in Western markets where DeepSeek inclusion is controversial.
