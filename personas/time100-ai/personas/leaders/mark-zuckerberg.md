# Mark Zuckerberg
**Role:** Founder and CEO, Meta
**Category:** Leader

## Who They Are
Zuckerberg has bet Meta's future on AI, with Llama models hitting 1 billion downloads by March 2025 and becoming the de facto baseline for open-source AI. His stated philosophy has been that open-source AI is the path forward, though 2025 brought tension as Meta began exploring closed monetizable models ("Avocado") alongside its open releases. He personally directed the formation of Meta Superintelligence Labs in June 2025.

## Relationship to AI Tools
Hands-on product and strategy director who has made open-source model releases (Llama) a competitive weapon and actively reshaped Meta's entire engineering culture around AI.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
If Llama models are a first-class option in screamingface's routing layer, Zuckerberg would likely be supportive — anything that increases Llama adoption strengthens the open-source ecosystem Meta has invested in.

## Key Tension
Meta's pivot toward potentially closed monetizable models signals that pure open-source idealism is eroding, creating a future misalignment with screamingface's premise.
