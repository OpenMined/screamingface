# Matthew Prince
**Role:** Co-founder and CEO, Cloudflare
**Category:** Leader

## Who They Are
Matthew Prince co-built Cloudflare into infrastructure for roughly one-fifth of the web, giving him a uniquely upstream vantage point on how AI is reshaping the internet's economics. He views AI as the next platform shift and has become one of tech's most vocal critics of AI power concentration, warning against a Medici-like scenario where a handful of AI labs control information flow. His consistent theme is that AI should be democratized across "500,000 companies spread around the world," not controlled by five.

## Relationship to AI Tools
Operator-class infrastructure builder; less a hands-on practitioner than a platform-layer strategist deeply invested in how AI traffic flows and who captures value from it.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
Prince would find the ensemble routing concept philosophically aligned — it's exactly the kind of infrastructure that distributes AI power rather than concentrating it. He'd likely appreciate OpenMined's open-source ethos, though his attention would be on network-level concerns (data routing, credit sharing protocols, privacy) rather than CLI ergonomics.

## Key Tension
His infrastructure worldview means he'd scrutinize the credit-sharing and routing layer's trust model more than the benchmark claims.
