# Sam Altman
**Role:** CEO, OpenAI
**Category:** Leader

## Who They Are
Sam Altman is the architect of OpenAI's ascent from research lab to dominant consumer AI company via ChatGPT. He is now openly focused on AGI and superintelligence, framing OpenAI's commercial success as the means to fund the safety research that makes beneficial AGI possible. His worldview is simultaneously bullish (AI will reshape every industry), safety-conscious, and commercially aggressive.

## Relationship to AI Tools
Non-technical executive operator — he doesn't write the models but sets the strategic direction and has become the public face of the AI era.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
Altman would see screamingface as a clever indie project but would notice it routes traffic away from OpenAI's models toward an ensemble. He'd be intellectually curious about the benchmark claims while privately viewing anything that commoditizes frontier model access as a mild competitive threat.

## Key Tension
OpenAI's closed API model and growing enterprise lock-in ambitions sit in direct tension with screamingface's premise that no single model wins.
