# Abhishek Singh
**Role:** Additional Secretary, Ministry of Electronics and IT (MeitY), India; CEO, IndiaAI Mission
**Category:** Shaper

## Who They Are
Abhishek Singh is a 1995-batch IAS officer who has led India's most significant digital infrastructure programs: DigiLocker, CoWIN (the COVID vaccination platform), Bhashini (language interfaces), and MyGov. He now leads the IndiaAI Mission with a goal of reaching 100 million users through AI-enabled public services, with 38,000 GPUs deployed across 14 private-sector partners.

## Relationship to AI Tools
A government systems architect with deep expertise in large-scale digital platform deployment. He understands AI at the infrastructure layer and is primarily focused on public service delivery and language accessibility.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
Singh would find the ensemble architecture interesting from a systems design perspective — routing between models based on capability is analogous to what IndiaAI does in a more centralized way. The local inference (Ollama) capability is directly relevant to India's digital sovereignty goals. He'd want to know whether screamingface can route to Bhashini-compatible or Indian-language models.

## Key Tension
India's AI strategy is explicitly about building and using Indian-developed or Indian-licensed AI infrastructure — screamingface's default model roster (US commercial APIs + open weights) partially aligns with India's open-source preference but bypasses the domestic capacity-building goal.
