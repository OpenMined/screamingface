# Alex Bores
**Role:** New York State Assemblymember (D-73); author of the RAISE Act
**Category:** Shaper

## Who They Are
Alex Bores is a Cornell ILR graduate with a Georgia Tech computer science master's and a former Palantir data scientist who won a state assembly seat in 2022. He authored the RAISE Act (Responsible AI Safety and Education Act), signed into law in December 2025 — the most far-reaching US state AI safety legislation, requiring companies that spend $100M+ training frontier models to have safety plans and report critical incidents. He's now running for Congress and is being targeted by an AI industry super PAC with $100M+ in backing.

## Relationship to AI Tools
Rare: a legislator with genuine technical depth. His Palantir background means he understands how large-scale AI systems are deployed in practice, not just in theory. He's a builder-turned-regulator.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
Bores would engage with screamingface more substantively than most legislators. The open-source, safety-minded framing resonates with his legislative priorities. He'd probe whether ensemble routing creates new classes of risk (e.g., a routing layer that bypasses model-level safety controls) and whether OpenMined's governance structure is compatible with the RAISE Act's safety plan requirements for large training runs.

## Key Tension
The RAISE Act targets companies spending $100M+ on training — screamingface doesn't train models, it routes between them — so Bores' legislation is orthogonal, but he'd still want to understand what routing-layer responsibility looks like in the next legislative cycle.
