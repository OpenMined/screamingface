# Bosun Tijani
**Role:** Minister of Communications, Innovation and Digital Economy, Nigeria
**Category:** Shaper

## Who They Are
Bosun Tijani is a Nigerian-British serial entrepreneur (co-founded CcHUB in 2010) with a PhD in innovation and economic development who was appointed Nigeria's tech minister in 2023. He launched Nigeria's National AI Strategy in April 2025, is building a national AI trust fund, and running an AI scaling hub with the Gates Foundation for healthcare/agriculture/education. He brings a startup mentality to government at continental scale.

## Relationship to AI Tools
A practitioner-turned-minister with genuine tech instincts. He thinks about AI infrastructure at national scale — not developer tooling specifically, but he understands the developer layer as foundational.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
Tijani would find the local-inference capability (Ollama) genuinely compelling given Nigeria's connectivity constraints and data sovereignty concerns. The open-source ethos aligns with his national AI strategy's emphasis on domestic capacity. He'd immediately ask whether screamingface works well in low-bandwidth environments and whether it can route to locally-hosted African language models.

## Key Tension
screamingface's current model roster is entirely Western (Claude, Gemini, Codex, Ollama with Western base models) — meaningful adoption in Tijani's strategic vision would require routing to models trained on African languages and local data.
