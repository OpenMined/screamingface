# Bruce Reed
**Role:** Head of Common Sense AI; formerly White House Deputy Chief of Staff for Policy (Biden)
**Category:** Shaper

## Who They Are
Bruce Reed was Biden's "AI Whisperer" — the White House Deputy Chief of Staff who engineered voluntary AI safety commitments from major tech companies and the sweeping Biden AI Executive Order. He chaired the White House AI Council. A career Democratic policy veteran, he moved to Common Sense Media in March 2025 to lead Common Sense AI, a child-safety-focused AI policy organization.

## Relationship to AI Tools
A policy architect and political operative, not a technologist. His AI engagement is focused on governance and consumer protection, particularly for children and families.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
Reed would view screamingface as well outside his current focus — a developer CLI tool has no meaningful child safety dimension and no consumer-facing risk surface he'd need to evaluate. He'd appreciate the local-first, privacy-preserving architecture in principle but have no professional reason to engage deeply.

## Key Tension
screamingface's risk surface is essentially zero on the dimensions Reed cares most about (child safety, misinformation, consumer harms), making him an unlikely ally or critic.
