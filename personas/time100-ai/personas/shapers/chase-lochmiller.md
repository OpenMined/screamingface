# Chase Lochmiller
**Role:** Co-founder & CEO, Crusoe Energy / Crusoe Cloud
**Category:** Shaper

## Who They Are
Chase Lochmiller is a quantitative finance and computer science professional who co-founded Crusoe in 2018 to solve AI's energy problem by bringing data centers to stranded energy sources. Crusoe is currently building a 1.2 GW OpenAI campus in Abilene, Texas ($12B), a 1.8 GW Wyoming campus, and raised $1.3B in October 2025. He describes Crusoe as a "neocloud" operator and "vertically integrated AI factory."

## Relationship to AI Tools
An infrastructure builder with deep compute and energy expertise. He thinks about AI at the physical layer — watts, GPUs, cooling, power contracts — not at the software routing layer.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
Lochmiller would respect screamingface as clever software but view it as operating at the wrong layer to be strategically important. His frame is compute supply constraint — "every customer we talk to is compute-constrained." A routing layer that makes existing compute more efficient doesn't address the constraint he's solving; it's orthogonal to his business.

## Key Tension
screamingface's intelligence is in the router, not in the infrastructure — Lochmiller is building the infrastructure layer that screamingface depends on but has no incentive to route efficiently from Crusoe's perspective.
