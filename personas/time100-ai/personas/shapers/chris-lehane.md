# Chris Lehane
**Role:** Chief Global Affairs Officer, OpenAI
**Category:** Shaper

## Who They Are
Chris Lehane is a veteran Democratic political operative — Clinton White House alum, Al Gore's press secretary, Airbnb's crisis communications chief — who joined OpenAI as its top policy executive. He authored OpenAI's submission to the Trump White House on AI policy, helped create a pro-AI super PAC (Leading the Future) to fight state-level AI regulations, and has pivoted OpenAI from "we'd welcome regulation" to explicit accelerationism.

## Relationship to AI Tools
A political strategist, not a technical person. He shapes the narrative and policy environment for the most powerful AI lab in the world — his job is to ensure OpenAI wins the political battles that determine the competitive landscape.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
Lehane would be professionally interested and personally wary. screamingface literally routes prompts away from any single provider — including OpenAI — which is structurally threatening to OpenAI's moat. At the same time, the "OpenAI as one model in an ensemble" positioning could be acceptable if it drives broader adoption. His reaction would be entirely strategic.

## Key Tension
screamingface's core value proposition — no single model wins, the ensemble beats them all — is a direct challenge to OpenAI's "frontier model superiority" brand narrative that Lehane is paid to protect.
