# Clara Chappaz
**Role:** Ambassador for Digital and AI, France (formerly Minister Delegate for AI and Digital Technologies)
**Category:** Shaper

## Who They Are
Clara Chappaz was France's first-ever AI minister, appointed September 2024 — a Harvard MBA and former head of the French Tech Mission who came from startup and e-commerce backgrounds. She launched France's National Institute for AI Evaluation and Safety (INESIA) and the "Dare AI" national adoption plan through 2030. In 2025, with government transitions, she moved to a role as Ambassador for Digital and AI.

## Relationship to AI Tools
A policy entrepreneur with a startup mentality. More operationally inclined than typical bureaucrats; she's shaped policy to drive adoption rather than just govern risk. Technically literate but not an engineer.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
Chappaz would be genuinely interested in screamingface as an example of AI adoption driving productivity — her "Dare AI" mandate was explicitly about accelerating AI use across France's economy. The open-source ensemble model also aligns with French digital sovereignty concerns. She'd likely ask what French or European models are in the ensemble.

## Key Tension
French AI policy has historically prioritized European model champions (Mistral); screamingface's model-agnostic routing is good for sovereignty in principle but would need to demonstrably include European model options to satisfy her industrial policy interests.
