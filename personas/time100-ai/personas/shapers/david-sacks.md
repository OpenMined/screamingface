# David Sacks
**Role:** White House AI & Crypto Czar (Special Advisor for AI and Crypto); General Partner, Craft Ventures
**Category:** Shaper

## Who They Are
David Sacks is the Trump administration's AI and crypto policy czar, a PayPal Mafia veteran who served as PayPal's COO and later founded Yammer (acquired by Microsoft). His policies favor innovation over regulation, with explicit emphasis on open-source AI development and lowering barriers for startups. He leads the Presidential Council of Advisors for Science and Technology and is under scrutiny for potential conflicts given his vast AI investments.

## Relationship to AI Tools
An operator and investor, not a researcher. He shapes the policy environment more than products. His sympathy for open-source AI and startup accessibility makes him directionally aligned with developer-tool efforts.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
Sacks would likely view screamingface favorably as a concrete example of what open-source AI policy enables — a locally-run, multi-model ensemble that doesn't require picking a single corporate winner. The "American AI competitiveness" framing would land well. He'd have no particular interest in the OpenMined academic angle.

## Key Tension
Sacks operates at the federal policy layer; his actual influence on a developer CLI tool's adoption is indirect, and his own conflicts of interest in AI investments may make any endorsement fraught.
