# Doug Matty
**Role:** Former Pentagon Chief Digital and AI Officer (CDAO); now working on "Golden Dome" missile defense
**Category:** Shaper

## Who They Are
Doug Matty is a 30-year Army officer (West Point graduate) with an MIT doctorate in engineering systems who was appointed as the Pentagon's first permanent CDAO under the Trump administration in April 2025. In that role he oversaw $200M contracts with OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and xAI to accelerate Defense Department AI adoption. He departed the CDAO role in December 2025 to focus on the Golden Dome missile defense initiative.

## Relationship to AI Tools
A DoD systems integrator with security clearance-level requirements for everything. His relationship to AI tools is mediated by procurement processes, security requirements, and battlefield application — the antithesis of a curl-installable open-source CLI.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
Matty would be deeply skeptical of screamingface in any defense context — routing prompts through Claude Code, Gemini, Codex, and Ollama with no security boundaries or auditability is a non-starter for classified environments. In a personal capacity, he might appreciate the multi-model routing concept as analogous to resilient military systems.

## Key Tension
The entire security model of screamingface — open-source, internet-connected model routing, shared credits — is architecturally incompatible with the air-gapped, supply-chain-vetted requirements of any serious DoD AI program.
