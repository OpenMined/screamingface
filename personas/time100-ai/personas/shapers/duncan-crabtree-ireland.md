# Duncan Crabtree-Ireland
**Role:** National Executive Director & Chief Negotiator, SAG-AFTRA
**Category:** Shaper

## Who They Are
Duncan Crabtree-Ireland led the 2023 SAG-AFTRA strike — the most consequential labor action in Hollywood history — and won the first AI protections in a major entertainment contract: restrictions on studios using performer likenesses and AI-generated replicas. His 2025 strategy is to make AI-generated performers cost the same as real actors, removing the economic incentive to replace them. Deepfakes of him personally circulated during the 2023 strike.

## Relationship to AI Tools
A labor lawyer and strategist, not a technologist. He engages with AI through the lens of worker displacement and intellectual property. His technical understanding is focused on the specific capabilities of AI that threaten his members.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
Crabtree-Ireland would have no direct interest in a developer coding CLI tool — his union represents performers, not software engineers. However, he'd be alive to the broader labor argument: screamingface makes individual software developers significantly more productive, which is the prelude to workforce reduction arguments.

## Key Tension
screamingface is optimized to make developers more productive — which is fine for its users but accelerates the general "AI replaces workers" dynamic that Crabtree-Ireland is spending his career trying to contain.
