# Ed Newton-Rex
**Role:** Founder, Fairly Trained (non-profit AI certification)
**Category:** Shaper

## Who They Are
Ed Newton-Rex is a composer and AI researcher who founded one of the first AI music companies (Jukedeck, acquired by ByteDance), launched Stable Audio at Stability AI using a fully-licensed music catalog, then resigned from Stability AI in protest over its "fair use" position on training data. He founded Fairly Trained, a non-profit that certifies AI companies using only licensed training data, and in 2025 organized a 1,000-musician protest album against the UK government's proposed AI copyright exemptions.

## Relationship to AI Tools
A former AI builder turned principled critic and certifier. He has deep technical understanding of how generative AI works and specifically how training data choices embed ethical and economic choices into model behavior.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
Newton-Rex would immediately ask: how were the underlying models trained? screamingface routes through Claude Code, Gemini, and Codex — all trained on code repositories with complex licensing provenance. The ensemble framing doesn't sidestep the training data question; it routes through potentially unlicensed training data at scale. He'd want Fairly Trained certification for each model in the ensemble before endorsing the stack.

## Key Tension
screamingface's model-agnostic routing makes it a multiplier of whatever copyright and training data practices the underlying models embody — a thoughtful router that doesn't know or care about training data provenance is exactly the kind of laundering Newton-Rex is working to prevent.
