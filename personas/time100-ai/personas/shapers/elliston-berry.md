**Role:** Student activist; co-developer of AI harm awareness curriculum; advocate for the Take It Down Act

## Who They Are
Elliston Berry is a Texas teenager who at age 14 discovered classmates had used AI to generate and distribute non-consensual nude images of her. She and her mother campaigned at the federal level, working with Senator Ted Cruz on the Take It Down Act — the first US law to regulate AI-induced image harms — signed by President Trump in 2025 with Berry present. She is now developing an AI awareness curriculum for schools.
