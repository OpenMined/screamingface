# Fei-Fei Li
**Role:** Professor of CS, Stanford; Co-Director, Stanford HAI; Co-founder & CEO, World Labs
**Category:** Shaper

## Who They Are
Fei-Fei Li is the "Godmother of AI," the researcher behind ImageNet who catalyzed the deep learning revolution. She co-directs Stanford's Human-Centered AI Institute and in 2023 founded World Labs, focused on spatial intelligence. She was named TIME Person of the Year in 2025 and received the Queen Elizabeth Prize for Engineering. She advocates for science-based AI governance and public investment in academic AI research.

## Relationship to AI Tools
A researcher-turned-founder with deep technical credibility and a clear policy voice. She builds AI systems and shapes how society thinks about AI simultaneously — a dual role that makes her unusually influential.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
Li would likely appreciate the open-source, democratizing framing — it aligns with her stated concern about private-sector concentration of AI power. She'd want to know how the system handles model quality signals and whether it contributes back to benchmarking commons. The CLI-first, developer-centric nature means it operates at a layer below her current spatial intelligence focus.

## Key Tension
Her HAI work emphasizes human-centered design for broad populations — screamingface is currently a developer power tool, not a democratizing consumer product, which limits how far her enthusiasm would extend.
