# Henna Virkkunen
**Role:** Executive Vice President, European Commission (Tech Sovereignty, Security and Democracy)
**Category:** Shaper

## Who They Are
Henna Virkkunen is the EU's top tech official — overseeing the Digital and Frontier Technologies portfolio, chairing the EU's AI Project Group, and responsible for implementing the AI Act. A Finnish politician and former minister, she has committed to swift AI Act implementation while simultaneously pushing Europe to become an "AI continent" with a competitive industrial footing.

## Relationship to AI Tools
A regulator and policy architect. Technical depth is political rather than engineering. Her job is to make the AI Act operational while ensuring European companies aren't disadvantaged — a constant tension.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
Virkkunen would view screamingface through a compliance lens first. A system that routes prompts through multiple models raises data residency and AI Act classification questions. The open-source nature might qualify for lighter-touch regulation under the Act, which could be a genuine advantage. The EU angle would be incidental unless screamingface makes a European model routing story.

## Key Tension
The AI Act's transparency and documentation requirements — does an ensemble router that dynamically selects models trigger new compliance obligations neither user nor provider is equipped to handle?
