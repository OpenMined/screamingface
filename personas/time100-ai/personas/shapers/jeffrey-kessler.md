# Jeffrey Kessler
**Role:** Under Secretary of Commerce for Industry and Security (Bureau of Industry and Security, BIS)
**Category:** Shaper

## Who They Are
Jeffrey Kessler is the Trump administration's chief official for export controls on dual-use technology, including advanced AI chips. He oversees BIS, which controls which countries can access high-end GPUs and AI infrastructure — the central lever in the US-China AI competition. He has advocated for doubling export control officers stationed abroad and expanded funding to enforce chip restrictions.

## Relationship to AI Tools
A trade lawyer and national security official. His relationship to AI is entirely through the lens of hardware, supply chains, and geopolitical competition.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
Kessler would be indifferent to screamingface as a product. His world operates at the chip and infrastructure layer, not the software and routing layer. The only angle that might register is whether screamingface routes prompts to models running on infrastructure that touches restricted jurisdictions.

## Key Tension
screamingface's "use any available model" architecture — including Ollama-hosted models — raises the theoretical question of whether locally-run foreign models could route around export control regimes, though this is not screamingface's intent.
