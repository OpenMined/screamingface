# Joshua Kushner
**Role:** Founder & Managing Partner, Thrive Capital
**Category:** Shaper

## Who They Are
Joshua Kushner is a Harvard-trained venture capitalist who founded Thrive Capital and built it into a $25+ billion firm through early, concentrated bets on OpenAI, Anysphere (Cursor), Databricks, and Isomorphic Labs. He raised a $10 billion fund in early 2026 — Thrive's largest ever. He is Jared Kushner's brother, giving him unusual proximity to the Trump White House's tech agenda.

## Relationship to AI Tools
A pure-play investor with exceptional pattern recognition for AI compounding. Not a builder or researcher but one of the most consequential allocators of capital in AI, with direct stakes in the model providers screamingface routes through.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
Kushner would look at screamingface and immediately evaluate whether it creates or destroys value in his portfolio companies. A system that commoditizes model selection — making OpenAI one interchangeable option among many — is structurally hostile to his Cursor and OpenAI positions. However, if screamingface drives overall developer AI consumption up, all models in the ensemble benefit.

## Key Tension
Thrive's largest bets are on individual model providers; screamingface's "no single model wins" architecture is directionally in tension with the thesis that frontier model providers capture durable value.
