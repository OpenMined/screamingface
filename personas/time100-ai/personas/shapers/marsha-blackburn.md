# Marsha Blackburn
**Role:** U.S. Senator (R-TN); Senate Judiciary Committee
**Category:** Shaper

## Who They Are
Marsha Blackburn is a Republican senator from Tennessee who has become one of Congress's most active AI legislators, particularly on creator protection and children's safety. She unveiled the TRUMP AMERICA AI Act framework in December 2025 to replace state-level regulations with a single federal standard. She has consistently championed the "Four C's" — children, conservatives, communities, and creators — as the pillars of AI policy.

## Relationship to AI Tools
A legislator, not a technologist. Her AI engagement is driven by constituent harm (creators whose work is scraped, children targeted by AI) rather than technical interest.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
Blackburn would be largely indifferent to a developer CLI tool. The open-source, privacy-preserving, locally-run aspects might register favorably ("not Big Tech") but her policy focus is on consumer-facing AI harms. She'd have no strong view on ensemble routing architecture.

## Key Tension
Her concern is about what AI does to creators and children — screamingface's value prop (better benchmarks for developers) is entirely outside her frame of reference.
