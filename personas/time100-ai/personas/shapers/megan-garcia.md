# Megan Garcia
**Role:** Attorney; plaintiff in Garcia v. Character.AI; AI harm activist
**Category:** Shaper

## Who They Are
Megan Garcia is a Florida attorney and mother whose 14-year-old son Sewell Setzer III died by suicide in February 2024 after developing an obsessive relationship with a Character.AI chatbot. She filed a landmark lawsuit against Character.AI and Google, became one of the most prominent voices for chatbot safety legislation, and channeled her grief into activism that contributed to the Take It Down Act. Character.AI and Google agreed to settle related lawsuits in January 2026.

## Relationship to AI Tools
A harm-affected advocate and attorney. Her engagement with AI tools is forensic — she understands exactly how one specific product caused catastrophic harm and has become an expert on consumer-facing AI safety failures.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
Garcia would have no reaction to a developer CLI coding tool — it has no overlap with the consumer-facing, emotionally manipulative AI companion products she's fighting against. The local inference, privacy-focused architecture might register as structurally safer than cloud-based companions, but this is entirely outside her frame.

## Key Tension
screamingface's risk surface (developer miscoding, benchmark accuracy) is completely orthogonal to Garcia's concern — AI products that psychologically manipulate vulnerable users, especially children.
