# Milagros Miceli
**Role:** Research Lead, DAIR Institute; Research Group Lead, Weizenbaum Institute (Berlin)
**Category:** Shaper

## Who They Are
Dr. Milagros Miceli is an Argentine-German sociologist and computer scientist who studies the labor conditions and power dynamics behind AI data work. She made her name documenting the lives of underpaid data annotators in Kenya, Syria, Brazil, and Germany, and founded the Data Workers' Inquiry — an academic project that pays data workers €35/hour to publish research by and about themselves. She is a DAIR Institute researcher alongside Timnit Gebru and brings a postcolonial, labor-rights lens to AI systems.

## Relationship to AI Tools
A critical researcher with genuine technical depth. She builds systems and datasets as well as studying them, but her primary contribution is surfacing the hidden labor that makes AI work — and making the power dynamics visible.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
Miceli would immediately probe what screamingface's model ensemble conceals. Each model it routes to was built on training data produced by low-paid annotators in the Global South — the ensemble abstraction makes those labor conditions invisible. The "beats SOTA benchmarks" claim would prompt her to ask: SOTA as measured by whom, on what data, reflecting whose values?

## Key Tension
screamingface's clean developer UX is precisely the kind of abstraction that makes the labor, data, and power conditions of AI invisible to its users — the more seamlessly it works, the more it obscures what Miceli is trying to make legible.
