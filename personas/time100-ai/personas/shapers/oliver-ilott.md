# Oliver Ilott
**Role:** Interim Director General for AI, UK DSIT; previously Head of AI Safety Institute (AISI)
**Category:** Shaper

## Who They Are
Oliver Ilott is one of the most consequential AI policy officials in the UK government — he ran the AI Safety Institute from its founding, then was elevated to interim Director General for AI at the Department for Science, Innovation and Technology. A Cambridge English graduate who previously ran the PM's private office in No. 10 and served as a Brexit negotiator, he combines elite civil service pedigree with deep AI safety operational experience. He has overseen £240M in new AISI funding and a £15M alignment research fund.

## Relationship to AI Tools
A policy architect and civil servant with increasing operational exposure to frontier AI through the AISI's work testing and evaluating advanced models. Not a builder, but one of the few government officials who engages with model evaluations and safety research at a technical level.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
Ilott would find screamingface genuinely interesting from a safety architecture perspective — an ensemble system that routes based on benchmark performance raises novel questions about how safety properties compose across models. The AISI's focus on evaluations and red-teaming aligns with screamingface's benchmark-driven claims. He'd want to see the evaluation methodology scrutinized.

## Key Tension
The AISI's central concern is whether frontier AI safety properties are maintained at deployment — screamingface's routing layer may inherit the safety properties of its constituent models but could also route around model-level refusals or safety guardrails in ways none of the individual models would permit.
