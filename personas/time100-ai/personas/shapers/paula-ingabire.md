# Paula Ingabire
**Role:** Minister of ICT and Innovation, Rwanda
**Category:** Shaper

## Who They Are
Paula Ingabire is Rwanda's Minister of ICT and Innovation since 2018, one of Africa's most influential digital policy leaders. A computer engineer with an MIT master's degree in Systems Design and Management, she has driven Rwanda's national AI strategy, predicted AI could contribute 6% of Rwanda's GDP, and oversees a 90,000km fiber-optic rollout. She was a 2020 World Economic Forum Young Global Leader.

## Relationship to AI Tools
A builder of national digital infrastructure and policy frameworks. She thinks about AI at the population level — how it reaches the 500 million Africans without reliable digital access — not at the developer CLI level.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
Ingabire would be genuinely interested in the open-source framing and the ability to run AI locally via Ollama — reducing cloud dependency matters enormously for African digital sovereignty. However, the current product is a developer CLI tool with a SWE benchmark focus; that's far removed from her priority of AI for agriculture, healthcare, and education at population scale.

## Key Tension
Local inference (Ollama) is a genuine win for Ingabire's sovereignty goals, but the rest of screamingface's value prop requires a developer base and code-centric use cases that Rwanda's current digital economy is still building toward.
