# Peter Kyle
**Role:** Secretary of State for Business and Trade, UK (formerly Secretary of State for Science, Innovation and Technology)
**Category:** Shaper

## Who They Are
Peter Kyle is a Labour MP who served as the UK's Science and Technology Secretary before a 2025 cabinet reshuffle. He holds a doctorate in community development and came to AI with personal conviction after seeing AI diagnostic tools that he believed could have saved his mother's life. He drove the UK's "light touch but assertive" AI regulatory approach and a 7.5 million worker AI skills program.

## Relationship to AI Tools
A policy champion with personal conviction rather than technical depth. He's a builder of policy frameworks and public-private partnerships, not a developer, but he engages substantively with the technology.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
Kyle would find screamingface interesting as a concrete example of what light-touch AI regulation enables — a grassroots developer tool that improves capability without requiring regulatory approval. The economic development angle (better tools for developers = UK productivity) would resonate. The CLI-specific nature would need translation for his primarily policy audience.

## Key Tension
Kyle moved to Business and Trade, not Science; screamingface's natural UK government champion is whoever now runs the science brief, reducing his direct relevance.
