# Peter Thiel
**Role:** Co-founder, Palantir; Managing Partner, Founders Fund
**Category:** Shaper

## Who They Are
Peter Thiel is the contrarian technology investor who co-founded PayPal and Palantir, wrote *Zero to One*, and has shaped a generation of Silicon Valley founders. His protégés now fill senior positions in the Trump White House AI policy apparatus. He famously said "crypto is libertarian, AI is communist" — viewing AI as radically centralizing power in the hands of a few massive institutions.

## Relationship to AI Tools
An investor and thinker, not an engineer. He evaluates AI through the lens of geopolitics, power concentration, and monopoly formation — his concern is whether AI reinforces existing power structures or disrupts them.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
Thiel would find the anti-concentration angle resonant — routing through open and local models (Ollama) rather than depending on a single corporate provider aligns with his stated concern about AI centralization. However, the open-source credit-sharing model may strike him as naively utopian, and the OpenMined provenance would draw scrutiny given his general skepticism of collective/cooperative tech structures.

## Key Tension
Thiel's intellectual framework says centralizing AI is dangerous — but he's also a Palantir co-founder who profits from government AI concentration; screamingface would need to explain how open-source ensembles don't simply create new dependencies.
