# Randi Weingarten
**Role:** President, American Federation of Teachers (AFT)
**Category:** Shaper

## Who They Are
Randi Weingarten leads the AFT, a 1.8 million-member union of teachers and education workers. She has taken a pragmatic rather than purely oppositional stance on AI in education: in July 2025 she launched the National Academy for AI Instruction with Microsoft, OpenAI, and Anthropic, aiming to train 400,000 teachers over five years. She is actively negotiating data privacy and IP protections into tech company agreements.

## Relationship to AI Tools
A labor leader engaging AI through collective bargaining strategy, not technical use. She is negotiating the terms under which her members encounter AI tools — focused on job security, data privacy, and worker dignity.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
Weingarten would be largely indifferent to a developer CLI tool — her constituency is teachers, not coders. However, the data privacy angle (local inference, no data leaving your machine) would register favorably; this is exactly the kind of protection she's been negotiating into tech agreements.

## Key Tension
Weingarten's concern is AI in classrooms and its effect on the teacher-student relationship — a developer coding tool is entirely outside the harm surface she's managing.
