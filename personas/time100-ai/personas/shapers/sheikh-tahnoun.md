# Sheikh Tahnoun bin Zayed Al Nahyan
**Role:** UAE National Security Advisor; Chairman, G42, MGX, ADIA, ADQ, First Abu Dhabi Bank
**Category:** Shaper

## Who They Are
Sheikh Tahnoun is the UAE's most powerful AI investor, controlling over $1.4 trillion in assets across sovereign funds and directly chairing G42, the UAE's flagship AI company. He personally brokered the UAE's $1.4 trillion investment commitment to US AI infrastructure after a March 2025 White House dinner with Trump. Under his leadership, G42 partnered with Microsoft, OpenAI, Oracle, and NVIDIA to build Stargate UAE.

## Relationship to AI Tools
A sovereign capital allocator operating at the infrastructure and geopolitical layer of AI. He builds AI ecosystems at national scale, not individual tools. His technical engagement is strategic rather than hands-on.
