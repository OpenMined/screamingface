# Stuart Russell
**Role:** Professor of Computer Science, UC Berkeley; Founder, Center for Human-Compatible AI (CHAI)
**Category:** Shaper

## Who They Are
Stuart Russell is one of the world's foremost AI researchers, co-author of the definitive textbook *Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach*, and a leading voice on AI safety and existential risk. He founded CHAI at Berkeley to work on ensuring AI systems are provably beneficial to humans, and was elected Fellow of the Royal Society in 2025. In January 2025 he wrote that "the AGI race is a race towards the edge of a cliff."

## Relationship to AI Tools
A foundational theorist and builder who understands AI at a technical depth few match. His orientation is toward alignment and safety — less interested in what models can do today, more focused on what happens when they can do everything.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
Russell would be intellectually interested in the ensemble routing concept and genuinely sympathetic to open-source, non-concentrated AI distribution. He'd probe whether the system's routing decisions are explainable and whether there are accountability mechanisms when it chooses poorly. The "share AI credits with friends" social layer would strike him as clever but beside the point.

## Key Tension
Does screamingface's benchmark-beating claim rest on capability alone, or does it incorporate any consideration of reliability, interpretability, or safe failure modes?
