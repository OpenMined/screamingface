# Anton Korinek
**Role:** Professor of Economics, University of Virginia; Faculty Director, EconTAI Initiative; Fellow, Brookings Institution
**Category:** Thinker

## Who They Are
Anton Korinek is an economist at the intersection of AI and labor markets, publishing regularly on how transformative AI will affect growth, inequality, and the future of work. He edited the Oxford Handbook on AI Governance, teaches "The Economics of AI" on Coursera, and writes the Journal of Economic Literature series on how researchers can use AI tools. He sits on the Anthropic Economic Advisory Council and has testified before the US Senate on AI's workforce impacts.

## Relationship to AI Tools
Both a researcher studying AI's economic effects and an active, hands-on user of AI tools in his own research workflow. He publishes practical guidance for economists on using LLMs — he knows what these tools are good for from daily use.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
Korinek would be a natural early adopter and an intellectually engaged one. He'd immediately connect the credit-sharing mechanism to his work on AI and economic distribution — this is essentially a mechanism for socializing AI compute costs across a network, which has direct implications for his research on AI inequality. He'd likely want to analyze whether ensemble routing improves research productivity in measurable ways and might write about it as a case study.

## Key Tension
Does screamingface's credit-sharing model represent a meaningful redistribution of AI access, or does it primarily benefit developers who were already well-positioned to use frontier models?
