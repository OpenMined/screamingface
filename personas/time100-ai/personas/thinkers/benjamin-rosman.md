# Benjamin Rosman
**Role:** Professor, University of the Witwatersrand; Founding Director, MIND Institute; Co-founder, Deep Learning Indaba and Lelapa AI
**Category:** Thinker

## Who They Are
Benjamin Rosman is a robotics and AI researcher at the University of the Witwatersrand in Johannesburg who has become one of the most important figures in African AI development. He co-founded the Deep Learning Indaba, which runs machine learning summer schools in 47 African countries, and Lelapa AI, an AI company building technology for African languages and contexts. He believes AI development must be genuinely global to be beneficial.

## Relationship to AI Tools
Both a researcher and a community builder — he builds AI systems and also builds the ecosystems of people who can build AI systems. He'd evaluate screamingface through both a technical lens (decision-making and transfer learning are his specialties) and a global-access lens.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
Rosman would be interested but would immediately raise the question of access: the tool depends on API credits to multiple frontier model providers, several of which have inconsistent availability or prohibitive costs outside North America and Europe. The Ollama integration (local model running) would be the most compelling piece for him — it's the part that works without reliable API access.

## Key Tension
An ensemble tool that defaults to cloud frontier models fails the global-access test for the African developer communities Rosman has spent his career building.
