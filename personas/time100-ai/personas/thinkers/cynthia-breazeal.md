# Cynthia Breazeal
**Role:** Professor of Media Arts and Sciences, MIT; Dean for Digital Learning, MIT Open Learning; Director, MIT RAISE
**Category:** Thinker

## Who They Are
Cynthia Breazeal is the founding figure of social robotics — she built Kismet, the first robot designed for emotionally expressive social interaction with humans, as her PhD thesis. She co-founded Jibo (companion robot startup) and now leads MIT RAISE, focused on AI literacy for children and educators. Her work has always centered on how AI and robots fit into human social and emotional life — not just what they can do but how they make people feel.

## Relationship to AI Tools
Both a builder and a researcher, but her orientation is toward embodied, social, and educational AI rather than language models or CLI tools. She'd engage with screamingface as a human-AI interaction problem rather than a capabilities problem.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
Breazeal would appreciate the democratization angle — her MIT RAISE work is fundamentally about making AI accessible to more people. But screamingface's developer-CLI-and-benchmark framing would feel narrow to her; she thinks about AI across the full population, not just coders. She'd push on the onboarding and literacy dimensions: what does someone need to understand to use this responsibly?

## Key Tension
A CLI tool that routes across frontier models assumes a level of technical fluency that excludes the broad population Breazeal most cares about reaching.
