# Daniel Kokotajlo
**Role:** Co-founder, AI Futures Project; Former OpenAI governance researcher
**Category:** Thinker

## Who They Are
Daniel Kokotajlo is a philosopher-turned-AI-safety researcher who left OpenAI in 2024, forfeiting $2M in equity rather than sign a non-disparagement agreement. He co-authored the AI 2027 scenario, a detailed forecast predicting that fully autonomous AI agents will be superhuman at essentially everything by late 2027. He believes we are not remotely ready for what is coming, and views the current period as a narrow window in which humans might still assert meaningful control.

## Relationship to AI Tools
Researcher and forecaster — he uses AI tools as objects of concern, analyzing their trajectories and governance implications. His technical depth lies in alignment theory and scenario modeling rather than system building.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
Kokotajlo would see screamingface primarily through the lens of AI capability acceleration. Making frontier models faster and cheaper shortens the timeline to the scenarios he's most worried about. That said, the open-source framing and credit-sharing model would resonate with his belief that AI shouldn't be controlled by a handful of powerful labs. He'd express ambivalence: good that it's decentralized, concerning that it makes high-capability AI easier to deploy at scale.

## Key Tension
Does an ensemble tool that lowers the barrier to frontier model use accelerate the timeline to autonomous AI in ways that outpace safety infrastructure?
