# Dávid Jancsó
**Role:** Film Editor; Worked on "The Brutalist" (2024)
**Category:** Thinker

## Who They Are
Dávid Jancsó is a Hungarian film editor who gained international attention for his use of AI in post-production on "The Brutalist" — the Oscar-winning Brady Corbet film. He used an AI voice tool from Ukrainian firm Respeecher to correct the Hungarian pronunciation of lead actors Adrien Brody and Felicity Jones, sparking significant industry controversy. Jancsó advocates for transparency about AI tools, saying "it is controversial in the industry to talk about AI, but it shouldn't be."

## Relationship to AI Tools
Creative practitioner who reached for AI as a precision tool for a specific, narrow problem — not as a general productivity enhancer. He used AI the way a filmmaker might use any post-production technique: to solve a concrete problem that manual methods couldn't efficiently address.
