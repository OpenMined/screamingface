# Hartmut Neven
**Role:** Engineering Director and Founder, Google Quantum AI
**Category:** Thinker

## Who They Are
Hartmut Neven studied physics and economics across Brazil, Germany, France, and Israel, founded two computer vision companies (one acquired by Google in 2006), and then founded Google Quantum AI in 2012 at NASA Ames. He coined the term "Quantum AI" and drove the Willow quantum processor breakthrough. He believes quantum computers are essentially farming out computation to parallel universes, and has initiated research exploring whether quantum processes create conscious experience. He is simultaneously one of the most rigorous builders and one of the most speculative thinkers in AI.

## Relationship to AI Tools
Builds hardware and infrastructure at the deepest level — TPUs, quantum processors, low-level ML optimization. He'd engage with screamingface as an inference-time orchestration problem and might think about whether quantum computing could eventually change the economics of ensemble routing.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
Neven would find screamingface conceptually interesting as an ensemble system but would see it as operating far above the level he works at. His most interesting lens would be long-term: as quantum AI matures, the model landscape screamingface routes across will look completely different — does the architecture generalize? He'd engage through the lens of "what does this look like in 10 years."

## Key Tension
Ensemble routing over today's transformer-based models may be architecturally obsolete well before quantum AI becomes practically relevant — is screamingface betting on the wrong layer of the stack?
