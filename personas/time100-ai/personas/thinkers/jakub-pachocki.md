# Jakub Pachocki
**Role:** Chief Scientist, OpenAI
**Category:** Thinker

## Who They Are
Jakub Pachocki is a Polish theoretical computer scientist who joined OpenAI in 2017 and rose to Chief Scientist after Ilya Sutskever's departure in 2024. He was a lead architect of the GPT-4, o1, and o3 models, and is considered one of the primary drivers of OpenAI's reasoning model research. Sam Altman calls him "one of the greatest minds of our generation." He operates almost entirely in the research layer, rarely speaking publicly.

## Relationship to AI Tools
Frontier researcher and model builder — he creates the models that everyone else routes and orchestrates. He thinks at the level of training runs, architecture choices, and benchmark design, not product surfaces.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
Pachocki would be aware of and probably indifferent to screamingface at a product level — routing on top of OpenAI's models is a common pattern. But he might be quietly interested in the ensemble benchmark data, since it would provide signal on how o1/o3 perform relative to competitors in naturalistic coding workflows.

## Key Tension
What does screamingface's benchmark data reveal about relative model strengths that Pachocki's internal evals might not capture?
