# Jared Kaplan
**Role:** Co-founder and Chief Science Officer / Responsible Scaling Officer, Anthropic; Associate Professor, Johns Hopkins University
**Category:** Thinker

## Who They Are
Jared Kaplan is a theoretical physicist who co-authored the 2020 Scaling Laws paper that gave the entire AI industry a quantitative framework for predicting model capability as a function of compute, data, and parameters. He co-founded Anthropic and now serves as both its Chief Science Officer and Responsible Scaling Officer — the person responsible for Anthropic's internal safety tripwires and capability thresholds. His lens is fundamentally empirical and quantitative; he believes you can measure your way to safety.

## Relationship to AI Tools
Deep technical researcher and organizational architect. He builds the frameworks that govern how frontier models are built and deployed — uses AI as a research object and policy instrument simultaneously.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
Kaplan would engage with the benchmark methodology seriously — he literally wrote the book on how to measure model capability, and he'd scrutinize whether the HLE benchmark is measuring genuine reasoning or benchmark overfitting. He'd be genuinely interested in how ensemble routing interacts with scaling laws: does combining weaker models at inference time approach the performance of a single stronger model? The Claude integration gives him a personal stake.
