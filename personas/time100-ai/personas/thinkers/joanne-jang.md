# Joanne Jang
**Role:** Head of Model Behavior, OpenAI
**Category:** Thinker

## Who They Are
Joanne Jang shapes how ChatGPT and other OpenAI models behave by default — what they will and won't do, and how they navigate the massive gray zone in between. She was "radicalized" into viewing the model itself as the product when she encountered a pre-release ChatGPT in 2022. She believes AI lab employees should not be the arbiters of what people can and can't create, and pushes for user empowerment up to the hard limits of harm.

## Relationship to AI Tools
Practitioner and product thinker operating at the intersection of AI behavior, ethics, and UX. Deeply technical in behavior policy, less so in systems or infrastructure.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
Jang would be immediately intrigued by the model-routing problem — her entire job is thinking about what models should and shouldn't do, and an ensemble that routes by task has implicit behavior-policy implications. She'd ask hard questions about whose guardrails apply when mixing models: if one model in the ensemble is more permissive than another, what does the ensemble's behavior look like?

## Key Tension
When you route a prompt across Claude, Gemini, and Ollama, whose content policy governs the response — and does screamingface need a behavior layer of its own?
