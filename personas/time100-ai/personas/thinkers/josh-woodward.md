# Josh Woodward
**Role:** VP, Google Labs; Head, Gemini App
**Category:** Thinker

## Who They Are
Josh Woodward joined Google in 2009 and spent 15 years building things that turn research into products — Chromebooks, Next Billion Users, and then Google Labs, where he shepherded NotebookLM, Project Mariner, and Google Flow from experiments to products. In 2025 he was given simultaneous leadership of both Google Labs and the Gemini app (450M+ monthly active users). He is a product leader and operator, not a researcher — his instinct is toward user adoption, iteration speed, and market timing.

## Relationship to AI Tools
Builder and product executive who thinks about AI through distribution, retention, and feature velocity. Deeply familiar with the model landscape from the inside; evaluates screamingface through a competitive product lens.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
Woodward would see screamingface as both a competitive signal and a product idea worth studying. An ensemble tool that routes to Gemini when it's strongest is essentially free distribution for his models. He'd be curious about the routing data — what tasks does Gemini win, and what does it lose? The credit-sharing mechanism might look like a distribution model worth borrowing.

## Key Tension
If screamingface's routing data shows Google's models losing to competitors on common coding tasks, that's more useful internal signal for Woodward than any external benchmark.
