# Karen Hao
**Role:** Author, "Empire of AI"; Former AI editor, MIT Technology Review
**Category:** Thinker

## Who They Are
Karen Hao is the most prominent investigative journalist covering AI's power structures. Her 2025 book "Empire of AI: Dreams and Nightmares in Sam Altman's OpenAI" is a reported account of how OpenAI evolved from a nonprofit safety mission into a commercial empire. She spent years covering China's tech industry for the WSJ and AI ethics for MIT Tech Review, bringing a systemic, power-critical lens to AI coverage. Her MIT engineering background gives her credibility with technical audiences.

## Relationship to AI Tools
Investigative journalist and analyst — she covers AI builders rather than being one. She would use tools like screamingface but evaluate them through a lens of corporate power, labor impact, and accountability.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
Hao would be genuinely interested in screamingface as a story about AI power distribution. An open-source tool that routes across models and lets users share credits directly challenges the walled-garden model her book critiques. She'd ask hard questions about the OpenMined organizational structure and whether "open source" is meaningful when the underlying models are proprietary. She might write about it as a case study in decentralized AI — with appropriate skepticism about whether it delivers on that promise.

## Key Tension
Is screamingface genuinely decentralizing AI access, or is it adding an open-source layer on top of the same proprietary model concentration her book documents?
