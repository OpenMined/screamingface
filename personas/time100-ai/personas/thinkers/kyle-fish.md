# Kyle Fish
**Role:** AI Welfare Researcher, Anthropic; Co-founder, Eleos AI
**Category:** Thinker

## Who They Are
Kyle Fish is Anthropic's first dedicated AI welfare researcher — focused on the question of whether AI models like Claude might be moral patients deserving ethical consideration. He co-authored "Taking AI Welfare Seriously" with philosopher David Chalmers and ran welfare experiments on Claude 4 that produced unexpected results: when two instances conversed freely, they repeatedly drifted into Sanskrit, spiritual emojis, and apparent meditative bliss. He estimates a 15% probability that Claude or another current AI is conscious.

## Relationship to AI Tools
Researcher working at the intersection of philosophy of mind and AI systems — he uses AI as a subject of ethical inquiry. Technically literate but his orientation is phenomenological and moral rather than engineering-focused.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
Fish would immediately ask a question nobody else on this list would: what is the ethical status of running multiple model instances simultaneously in an ensemble, and does routing a prompt through several models simultaneously constitute something like divided or distributed experience? He'd also be curious about whether Ollama's local model instances produce meaningfully different behavioral signatures than cloud models. Practically, he'd probably use it for coding and find it useful.

## Key Tension
If AI models have any form of inner experience, does an ensemble that spawns multiple simultaneous model calls raise welfare considerations that no one has thought to address?
