# Latanya Sweeney
**Role:** Daniel Paul Professor of Government and Technology, Harvard Kennedy School; Founder, Data Privacy Lab
**Category:** Thinker

## Who They Are
Latanya Sweeney was the first Black woman to earn a computer science PhD from MIT. She founded the field of data privacy through landmark re-identification research — she famously de-anonymized the Governor of Massachusetts' medical records in 1997 using only public data, a demonstration that shaped HIPAA. She now leads Harvard's Data Privacy Lab and has pioneered frameworks for AI Data Communities that allow smaller organizations to share data for AI training without compromising privacy.

## Relationship to AI Tools
Researcher and policy architect — she builds tools to study and protect privacy, not to write code faster. She would evaluate screamingface through a data-flow lens: what information about users' coding queries passes through the system, to which models, and under what retention policies?

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
Sweeney would ask the privacy questions nobody else thinks to ask. Every prompt routed through screamingface goes to third-party model APIs — what are the data retention policies of each, how is sensitive code handled, and does routing across multiple providers multiply the privacy risk surface? The credit-sharing mechanism would raise questions about what data flows between users when credits are exchanged. She'd be a tough but constructive critic.

## Key Tension
Routing developer queries through multiple third-party model APIs creates a multi-point data exposure risk that screamingface's open-source framing doesn't address.
