# Miles Congreve
**Role:** Chief Scientific Officer, Isomorphic Labs (Google DeepMind)
**Category:** Thinker

## Who They Are
Miles Congreve spent three decades in pharmaceutical drug discovery before joining Isomorphic Labs — the DeepMind spinout built on AlphaFold — where he now serves as CSO. He has helped design 25 clinically evaluated drugs and co-invented a breast cancer treatment. He represents a rare bridge between traditional experimental pharmaceutical science and AI-native drug discovery, compressing a year of molecular design work into seconds.

## Relationship to AI Tools
A domain scientist using AI as a discovery instrument. He is not a software developer and doesn't work in coding pipelines — his AI tool usage is highly specialized (AlphaFold, molecular design systems) rather than general-purpose coding environments.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
Congreve would be largely outside screamingface's target audience. He might be curious about it as a concept — an ensemble that routes tasks to best-fit models resonates with how drug discovery increasingly combines multiple AI approaches. But practically, it's a developer tool and he's a scientist. He'd applaud the open-source, collaborative spirit.

## Key Tension
Screamingface's value proposition is entirely in the coding domain; Congreve's AI workflows are in computational biology, creating almost zero practical overlap despite conceptual resonance.
