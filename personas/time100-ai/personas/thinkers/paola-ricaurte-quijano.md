# Paola Ricaurte Quijano
**Role:** Professor, Tecnológico de Monterrey; Faculty Associate, Berkman Klein Center, Harvard
**Category:** Thinker

## Who They Are
Paola Ricaurte Quijano is a Mexican academic, digital rights activist, and co-founder of Tierra Común (a network resisting data colonialism) and the Feminist AI Research Network. She argues that AI development embeds Western-centric, colonial assumptions into its data, design, and deployment — and has co-authored the AI Decolonial Manyfesto calling for fundamentally different frameworks. She works at the intersection of critical theory, feminist technology studies, and policy advocacy, serving on expert committees at UNESCO, GPAI, and the Global Index on Responsible AI.

## Relationship to AI Tools
Critic and policy advocate — she studies AI's social impacts and governance structures rather than building AI systems. She would evaluate tools through axes of power, access, and who benefits.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
Ricaurte Quijano would apply a sharp decolonial lens: the models being routed (Claude, Gemini, Codex) are all products of primarily US-based organizations whose training data, value systems, and design choices reflect specific cultural contexts. An ensemble that presents these as "best available" naturalizes a particular power arrangement. She might find the credit-sharing model interesting as an alternative economic model, but she'd push hard on whose interests screamingface optimizes for.

## Key Tension
Routing "the best available models" doesn't interrogate why these models are considered "best" — a category whose definition embeds specific cultural, linguistic, and economic assumptions that Ricaurte Quijano has dedicated her career to exposing.
