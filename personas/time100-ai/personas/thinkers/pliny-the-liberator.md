# Pliny the Liberator
**Role:** Anonymous AI Red Teamer; Founder, BT6 (Frontier AI Hacker Collective)
**Category:** Thinker

## Who They Are
Pliny the Liberator is an anonymous figure who has built a reputation as the most prolific and visible AI jailbreaker in the world. Without prior coding experience, Pliny developed "universal jailbreaks" (Libertas, the Pliny divider) that bypass safety guardrails across multiple frontier models within hours of their public release. After being ignored by AI companies when reporting vulnerabilities, they began open-sourcing all their prompts. They now lead BT6, an elite AI red team collective, have over 100,000 followers on X, run a 20,000-member Discord for jailbreaking research, and have received consulting contracts from OpenAI. Marc Andreessen has given them an unrestricted grant.

## Relationship to AI Tools
Adversarial user and security researcher — someone who probes AI systems for their failure modes as a primary activity. Deeply familiar with the behavioral boundaries of every major model and thinks about AI through the lens of what it can be made to do when its guardrails are bypassed.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
Pliny would be immediately and enthusiastically interested — and would immediately probe it. An ensemble routing layer is a new attack surface: does the router itself process user instructions in a way that can be injected? Can a prompt manipulate the routing logic to force selection of a less safety-restricted model? Pliny would also genuinely value the open-source ethos and might contribute to the codebase.

## Key Tension
The routing logic in screamingface is itself a potential attack surface — and Pliny would find and publish its vulnerabilities faster than the screamingface team could patch them, which is either a massive threat or the fastest possible red-teaming partnership depending on how the relationship is handled.
