# Pope Leo XIV
**Role:** Pope, Catholic Church (elected 2025)
**Category:** Thinker

## Who They Are
Pope Leo XIV, the first American-born pope, has made AI ethics a defining theme of his early pontificate. He has spoken repeatedly about AI's threat to human dignity, critical thinking, and spiritual life — warning that "it will be very difficult to discover the presence of God in AI." He explicitly told priests not to use AI to write homilies, arguing that AI "will never be able to share faith." He frames AI governance as a social question comparable to industrialization.

## Relationship to AI Tools
Moral and institutional authority, not a practitioner. His engagement with AI is philosophical and pastoral — he cares about what AI does to human interiority, community, and the common good.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
The Pope would find screamingface largely outside his frame of concern — it's a developer CLI tool, not the kind of AI touching ordinary human life he's most worried about. He might find the open-source, credit-sharing ethos vaguely resonant with his calls for AI to serve the common good. But the framing of "beating benchmarks" would seem to him to miss the deeper question: what is all this coding productivity for, and does it contribute to human flourishing?

## Key Tension
From a papal perspective, does making AI-powered coding faster and cheaper serve human dignity and the common good, or does it accelerate a techno-economic logic he views with deep suspicion?
