# Regina Barzilay
**Role:** Dieter and Lori Chidsey Professor in Health Sciences and Technology, MIT CSAIL; AI Faculty Lead, MIT Jameel Clinic
**Category:** Thinker

## Who They Are
Regina Barzilay is a MacArthur Fellow and the first recipient of the AAAI Squirrel AI Award for Benefit of Humanity. Born in Moldova, she built a career in NLP before a 2014 breast cancer diagnosis redirected her toward AI for drug discovery and early disease detection. She developed AI systems that predict breast cancer risk up to five years in advance and has applied deep learning to antibiotic discovery and molecular design. Her work is defined by urgency — she knows what it means for the research to matter at a human level.

## Relationship to AI Tools
Deep technical builder who uses AI as a discovery instrument. She writes code, trains models, and publishes research — her orientation is scientific rather than engineering-infrastructure. She likely uses AI coding assistants to accelerate research work.
