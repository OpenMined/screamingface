# Ryoji Ikeda
**Role:** Artist and Composer; Based in Paris and Kyoto
**Category:** Thinker

## Who They Are
Ryoji Ikeda is one of the world's leading electronic musicians and media artists, known for large-scale installations that transform raw data — from NASA datasets to human genome sequences — into overwhelming sensory experiences of synchronized sound and light. He became a member of the influential Kyoto art collective Dumb Type in 1993, and has since exhibited at Art Basel, major museums worldwide, and CERN. He has collaborated with mathematicians at Harvard and conducted interdisciplinary research with physicists.

## Relationship to AI Tools
Engages with computation and data as raw aesthetic and philosophical material, not as a productivity instrument. He is probably one of the few people on this list who doesn't think about AI primarily in terms of capabilities or safety — he thinks about it as a phenomenon to be understood and rendered perceptible.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
Ikeda's reaction would be the most genuinely different from anyone else on this list. The data flows in screamingface — prompt routing metadata, model selection signals, token usage patterns across an ensemble — might be aesthetically interesting to him as a dataset for a future installation. He might find the ensemble architecture philosophically interesting: multiple models responding to the same prompt creates something like a chorus of different machine perspectives.

## Key Tension
Screamingface is built for productivity optimization; Ikeda's lens on computation is aesthetic and philosophical, making the tool's core value proposition largely irrelevant to his practice.
