# Xue Lan
**Role:** Professor and Dean, Schwarzman College, Tsinghua University; Director, Institute for AI International Governance (I-AIIG)
**Category:** Thinker

## Who They Are
Xue Lan is one of China's most prominent AI governance scholars and the architect of Tsinghua University's engagement with global AI policy. He directs China's National Expert Committee on Next Generation AI Governance, sits on the UN Committee of Experts on Public Administration, and leads the Internet Governance Forum's leadership panel. With a PhD in engineering and public policy from Carnegie Mellon, he bridges technical understanding and governance design, working to ensure Chinese institutions have a substantial voice in shaping international AI norms.

## Relationship to AI Tools
Policy researcher and institution builder — he studies AI governance rather than building AI systems. He would engage with screamingface primarily through questions of governance, standards, and geopolitical implications of open-source AI tool proliferation.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
Xue Lan would find screamingface interesting as a governance case study: a multi-model ensemble that routes across US-based frontier models represents a specific geopolitical configuration of AI dependency. He would likely ask whether the ensemble includes Chinese frontier models (Qwen, DeepSeek) and, if not, why not. The open-source framing would be interesting given China's own push toward open AI frameworks.

## Key Tension
An ensemble that defaults to US-based frontier models without including competitive Chinese models implicitly embeds a geopolitical hierarchy — a point Xue Lan would make directly and that screamingface's architecture doesn't address.
