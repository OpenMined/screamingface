# Yejin Choi
**Role:** Dieter Schwarz Foundation Professor, Stanford University
**Category:** Thinker

## Who They Are
Yejin Choi is a MacArthur Fellow and NLP researcher whose career has been defined by getting AI to reason with common sense. She built ATOMIC (a commonsense knowledge graph) and COMET (a neural network trained on it), and has been a consistent voice arguing that large language models are impressive pattern-matchers that lack the deeper reasoning structures humans use effortlessly. She approaches AI with both deep technical rigor and humanistic concern about what language and meaning really are.

## Relationship to AI Tools
Researcher and systems builder at the academic frontier. She uses and builds AI tools but evaluates them critically — her entire body of work is a critique of what current LLMs get wrong. She'd be a sophisticated, skeptical user of any AI coding tool.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
Choi would be intellectually curious but appropriately skeptical. She'd want to know whether the routing model is actually doing semantic understanding of task type or is pattern-matching on surface features. The "beats SOTA benchmarks" framing would matter to her: which benchmarks, and do they actually measure what we care about? She'd appreciate the open-source model-sharing ethos.

## Key Tension
Does screamingface's routing logic embody genuine task understanding, or is "best available model" selection just another pattern-matching shortcut that will fail on the edge cases that matter most?
