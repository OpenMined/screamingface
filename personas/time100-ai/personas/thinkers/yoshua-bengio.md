# Yoshua Bengio
**Role:** Professor, Université de Montréal; Founder, Mila; Founder, LawZero
**Category:** Thinker

## Who They Are
Yoshua Bengio is one of the three "Godfathers of AI" who won the 2018 Turing Award for foundational deep learning research. He has pivoted substantially toward AI safety advocacy, leading the first International AI Safety Report in 2025 and founding the nonprofit LawZero to develop "Scientist AI" — a non-agentic AI designed to verify the safety of other systems. He warns that superintelligence risks could materialize within 5–10 years.

## Relationship to AI Tools
Deep technical researcher who built the foundations the entire industry runs on. He engages AI tools as research objects, not instruments — his contribution is conceptual frameworks and safety proposals.

## Likely Reaction to screamingface
Bengio would appreciate the open-source, decentralized ethos — it aligns with his skepticism of power concentration in a few frontier labs. He'd likely be interested in how ensemble routing could reduce single-model failure modes. But his primary lens is catastrophic risk, and he'd push on whether screamingface's orchestration layer creates new attack surfaces or accelerates AI capability adoption faster than safety work can keep pace.

## Key Tension
Does democratizing access to multiple frontier models simultaneously increase or decrease the overall safety risk surface?
